                    On the watch

                    What the step screen shows on a Fenix during this session, step by step. Cues travel as step notes and appear when the step begins.

                      Missed a session?

                      Readapt moves or drops the sessions around it by rule — a missed quality session next to another one is dropped rather than stacked, a long run goes to the next free weekend day — and lists every change with its reason. Keep leaves the plan exactly as it is.

                      Pause the plan

                      Illness, a holiday, a niggle. Everything from the start date shifts by the number of days; the first week back is scaled down; sessions that would land after the race are dropped.

                      Back after a layoff

                      Stryd's published tiers: up to a week off, just resume; 8–14 days, restart the phase; 15–28, back to aerobic work; longer, start again at foundation. This builds the ramp for the tier.

                      Rewrite this session

                      The model rewrites just this one, keeping the rest of the week. Say what should change.
